i-nav Product

Four Pillars of the i-nav Platform

i-nav is positioned as an integrated ecosystem — not a point solution. All four pillars operate together.


01

TVOS Routing Algorithm

AI-driven multi-objective voyage optimisation. Simultaneously calculates ~100,000 routes across all objectives, presents top 3. Developed with Alan Turing Institute + University of Southampton over 15+ years.

  • TCE, minimal fuel, JITA, best time, safety, GHG — all at once
  • Genetic algorithm — future-proof, adapts to new regulations via AI
  • Calculates within seconds
02

Weather Intelligence — "The Optimal Model"

InfoPlaza's proprietary multi-model blend, enhanced with high-resolution in-house models and AI. Primary atmospheric strength.

  • 15-day forecast · 5-year hindcast
  • 50+ meteorological parameters
  • Storm tracks (live Nowcast layer)
  • Wind speed, wave heights, pressure
  • Ocean currents at 10m depth average
03

Navigation Database

Advanced and comprehensive navigation database ensuring all calculated routes are legally and operationally valid.

  • ENC-validated routes
  • IMO Traffic Separation Scheme (TSS) compliant
  • High-risk areas (HRA) & marine protected zones
  • DAI restricted areas
  • Draft-aware (set manually or from vessel model)
04

Compliance Suite

Full regulatory coverage — CII, EU ETS, FuelEU, EU/UK MRV, IMO DCS. Designed to reduce administrative burden.

  • CII rating projection 3 years ahead
  • EU ETS Wallet per vessel
  • FuelEU Pooling scenario planning
  • Charter Party reports
  • MRV + IMO DCS export-ready

Key Differentiator

TVOS: Multi-Objective AI Algorithm

InfoPlaza's stated key differentiator vs every other routing solution on the market. Developed over 15+ years with the Alan Turing Institute and University of Southampton. Delivered by partner Theyr.

Traditional (Single-Objective)

Optimises for one objective at a time. Combining multiple requires manual trade-off.

Time Charter Equivalent (TCE) Minimal Fuel Consumption JITA Best Time Safety GHG Emissions
Must be re-coded entirely to add a new objective (e.g. new regulation)

TVOS (Multi-Objective)

All objectives calculated simultaneously. ~100,000 routes evaluated → top 3 presented.

Time Charter Equivalent (TCE) Minimal Fuel Consumption JITA Best Time Safety GHG Emissions
AI-driven: adapts automatically to new regulations without re-coding. Future-proof.

ProSeaDure

Vessel Modelling & Operational Guidance

Delivered by partner ProSeaDure. Two distinct capabilities — one live today, one in development.


1 — Vessel Performance Model Live

Built per-client by ProSeaDure on request. Uses vessel particulars and historical voyage data to produce a routing-ready performance model. This is a professional services engagement — ProSeaDure builds it for each vessel, it is not self-serve out of the box.

Input

  • Vessel particulars
  • Operational profile & constraints
  • Historical noon / sensor data
  • Weather, waves & current
→

Modelling Layer

  • Speed / power / fuel curves
  • Weather-response layer (wind/wave impact)
  • Calibration & refinement with voyage data
→

Outcome

  • Routing-ready performance model
  • Feasible speed / power envelope
  • Basis for ETA, fuel & safety estimations


2 — Wave-to-Motion Prediction Dashboard In Development

A separate upcoming capability. Takes vessel-specific RAOs + directional wave spectrum to predict motion response (heave, roll, pitch) and provide GO/caution/no-go operational guidance. Two things are still in development here:

  • The motion prediction dashboard itself — not yet live
  • Integration of motion predictions into the routing engine — also still in development (will be a standalone tool first)

Input

  • Directional wave spectrum
  • Heading & speed
→

Vessel Response Model

  • Vessel-specific RAOs
  • Applied to wave spectrum
→

Motion Response

  • Heave / roll / pitch
  • By weather and speed
→

Operational Insight

  • Compare headings, speeds & windows
  • Heading sweep polar diagram
  • GO / caution / no-go guidance
  • 3-day operational forecast

Gaps & Limitations

Known Gaps / In Development

Confirmed directly in the webinar — from Q&A responses or presenter caveats.


Ocean Currents: 10m Depth Average Only

Confirmed by Menno in closing Q&A. i-nav uses ocean currents as a 10m depth average parameter. No tidal component breakdown, no full depth profile stated.

Wave-to-Motion Prediction Dashboard Not Yet Live

Nikki confirmed: this is a separate upcoming feature from the core vessel modelling — currently in development. When live, it will be a standalone dashboard, not integrated into the routing engine. 3-day operational forecast only.

Wind Resistance / Rort: Data-Dependent

During Q&A, neither Menno nor Nikki could confirm wind area and rort (wind resistance coefficients) are handled out-of-the-box. Answer: "depends on the data you provide."

Vessel Motion & Routing: Not Integrated

Vessel motion guidance and voyage optimisation operate as two separate tools. The optimisation algorithm is not yet aware of vessel motion constraints.

Consortium Dependency

i-nav is a three-party product. InfoPlaza controls the weather layer; Theyr controls the algorithm; ProSeaDure controls the front-end and hardware. Product roadmap dependent on all three partners aligning.

Where Each Wins

Amphitrite
  • Best-in-class ocean current data — 2–3.5km AI resolution
  • HIRES-CURRENTS-Net: physics-informed AI fusing SSH, SST and chlorophyll satellite data
  • Has a data API for 3rd party integration
  • Full vessel modelling alongside precision current routing
InfoPlaza / i-nav
  • Most complete compliance suite (CII, EU ETS, FuelEU, MRV, IMO DCS)
  • Only one with onboard hardware — automated data collection, LTE/Starlink/VSAT failover
  • 15-day forecast, 50+ parameters, 5-year hindcast
  • Largest organisation — 120 staff, PE-backed, 5,000 existing clients
MWI
  • Weather uncertainty routing — accounts for all possible weather scenarios simultaneously
  • First to map and alert on maritime weather fronts (extreme weather zones)
  • 30 years of offshore racing expertise encoded into the AI (Christian Dumard)
  • ESA satellite + computer vision for real-time storm zone detection

What Each Is Missing

Amphitrite
  • No dedicated compliance system — documented voyage only, no CII/EU ETS tooling
  • No onboard hardware or sensor integration
  • No human expert support layer
  • Weather uncertainty routing not a core focus
InfoPlaza / i-nav
  • Ocean currents = 10m depth average only — no precision hydrodynamic layer
  • Closed platform — no data API for 3rd party integration
  • Wave-to-motion prediction dashboard still in development — separate from core vessel modelling, not yet integrated into routing
MWI
  • No proprietary ocean current data at all
  • No vessel modelling
  • No compliance system
  • Grant-funded only, ~15 people — no Series A yet
